# Direction contract: Deep Teal Editorial (user-pinned)

Pinned by Jonathan: keep the five brand colors, the name and the logo; everything else is free; direction "Deep Teal Editorial". A user-pinned direction beats any dice roll, so no concept-seed roll was run (the Impeccable launcher binary was not available without a download). Build path: code-led (no image generation here). Seed: none (unavailable).

THESIS: Frame, then Scale. The logo is one small rounded square growing into four circles, a lens-ring scale. The site is built as that instrument: rings and measured steps carry structure, so the page refuses the category template of a photo hero over three equal icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep teal #264653 full-bleed grounds with aqua #68b3b5 ring hairlines. Cool paper #f3f7f7 for reading, ink #0b1f24 for text. Teal #17788e as the action color on paper, aqua fill with deep text as the action color on deep. Bricolage Grotesque for display (optical-size and width axes, tight tracking), Hanken Grotesk for text. 8px radii echoing the logo's square, hairline rules, grain on deep bands only. Bans: eyebrow labels above headings, gradient text, nested cards, hard offset shadows, equal-card icon grids as page structure.

STORY: An owner of an independent optical practice learns within seconds that this is a consultant who understands optical retail, believes it because of the MBA and the years on the floor, and books a call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed deep teal. Left seven columns: the H1 at display scale, a two-sentence lead, two actions (Book A Free Call in aqua, See How I Work as a text link). Right five columns: concentric rings built from the logo's geometry that draw themselves, with Jonathan's portrait seen through the innermost ring like a lens aperture. A credential line sits on a ruler-tick hairline at the bottom edge of the viewport.

FORM: Lens and ruler, from the practice's own world (optical measurement), position 1 of a pinned direction. seed: none (unavailable).
