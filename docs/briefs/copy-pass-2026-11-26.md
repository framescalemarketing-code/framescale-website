## 1. Goal
- **Who it is for:** an independent optical practice owner who lands on the site from a search or from an AI assistant's answer.
- **What they can do afterwards:** read a plain headline, see in one glance that Jonathan has worked the lab, the sales floor and a store manager's desk, and see a real client (On-Sight Safety Optics) he built the website and marketing for.

## 5. SEO and AEO plan (written for the On-Sight kind of customer, on independent practices' pages)
- The queries stay "optical practice marketing consultant", "optical marketing", "marketing for opticians". The case study page owns the proof query: "optical business website and marketing case study" and the name "On-Sight Safety Optics".
- **AEO shape:** question headings that match how owners ask; the answer in the first sentence; consistent names (Jonathan Mejia, FrameScale, optical practice); one fact per sentence; FAQPage JSON-LD text identical to the visible text.
- **Schema:** the case study gets a WebPage node with `about` pointing to an Organization node for On-Sight (name and url only), plus Breadcrumb. Person `knowsAbout` gains "Optical lab operations". Nothing claims an address, rating or review.
- **Voice:** first person, Jonathan, spoken. The humanizer rules apply: no "not X but Y", no triads for rhythm, no one-line closers, no em or en dashes.

## 6. Edge and failure cases

- A figure on On-Sight's site changes: copy carries the date it was read. He is told to recheck before launch.
- A detail Jonathan has not confirmed: written as a bracketed `[NEED: ...]` only in the review list below, never on a page.
- Long headings at 375 px: check for wrap and overflow.
- Insights drafts stay unpublished and keep their gate.
