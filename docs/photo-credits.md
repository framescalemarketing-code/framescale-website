# Photo credits

All photography on this site is Jonathan's own. There is no stock imagery.

| File | Subject | Where it is used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `public/photos/founder/jonathan-about.jpg` | Portrait | Home hero, about hero, `Person.image` in JSON-LD |
| `public/photos/story/rayban-frame.webp` | Black Ray-Ban optical frame, low angle | About → chapter 01, "In The Back Of The Shop" |
| `public/photos/story/cartier-rimless.webp` | Gold rimless Cartier frame, panther temple detail | Optical → the "I Have Worked Your Side Of The Counter" band |

Each frame appears on exactly one page. They are the only two frame
photographs in the repo, so using both in both places would make a four-page
site look like it owns two pictures.

The two frame photographs date from the optical years and were originally shot
for the retired industry pages. Both were normalized before committing: sRGB,
EXIF and ICC stripped, longest edge capped at 2400px, re-encoded to WebP q84.
Together they are 0.42MB, down from 4.50MB as camera JPEGs.

Both frames carry visible maker marks. They are photographs of product handled
during that job, not a claim of any current relationship with either brand.
